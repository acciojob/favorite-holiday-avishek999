Goa


Goa is a beautiful holiday destination in India, famous for its stunning beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese heritage, and delicious food.

  1. Baga Beach Famous for water sports and nightlife.
  2. Fort Aguada – A historic Portuguese fort with beautiful sea views.
  3. Basilica of Bom Jesus – A famous historic church in Old Goa.
  4. Dudhsagar Falls – A spectacular waterfall surrounded by greenery.
  5. alangute Beach C– One of Goa's most popular beaches.
  • Goan Fish Curry – A spicy and flavorful coconut-based curry.
  • Prawn Balchão – Tangy and spicy prawns cooked with rich masala.
  • Chicken Cafreal – Chicken marinated in a flavorful green herb paste.
